# Contributing to RawFish

Contributors will propose changes to this repository after accepting the contributor agreement below, and each change is held to the engineering standard that follows. RawFish is not accepting outside contributions yet: the agreement and its acceptance mechanism need legal review before public contribution intake opens.

## Contribution rights

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Before RTEPL accepts a contribution, the contributor must accept the separate [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). That agreement gives RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited permanent project-use, distribution, sublicensing, and relicensing rights, including the right to offer the combined project under commercial terms without later renegotiation or approval.

A Developer Certificate of Origin is not a substitute for this agreement. Accepted contributions follow the outbound licence of their owning source home:

- RawFish's modifications and additions use `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, as `LICENSE-ADDITIONS.md` states;
- upstream and third-party material retains its applicable licence.

## Engineering standard

A contribution must preserve the repository's existing ownership boundaries and describe only what exists. Build and check it as [the README](README.md#build-and-test), the [runbook](RUNBOOK.md#maintainer-qualification) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describe. Preserve third-party provenance and applicable notices.

RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited owns RawFish's modifications and additions and the commercial licensing authority over them; the upstream code remains its authors'. Contributors are acknowledged for accepted work; a patch does not automatically change the canonical author entry in `CITATION.cff`.

Use only project-owned or properly licensed names, imagery and other material. Do not imply endorsement by another project or rights holder.
